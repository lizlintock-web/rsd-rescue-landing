RSD Rescue · Day 5

The STOP Technique

Some spirals give you time. The ambiguous text message that sits unanswered for hours, the calendar invite you can stew over for a full day before the meeting even happens. These have room in them for a process like the one you learned yesterday: sit down, write out the four questions, work through the evidence methodically.

Other spirals don't give you that room at all. Someone says something in a meeting and you feel the floor tilt in real time, right there, with six other people watching and waiting for you to respond to whatever comes next. These are the spirals that arrive and demand a response in seconds, not hours, and a four-question written exercise is not going to help you in the middle of one. You need something faster. Something you can run standing up, mid-conversation, without anyone else in the room even noticing you're doing it.

That's what today's tool is for.
